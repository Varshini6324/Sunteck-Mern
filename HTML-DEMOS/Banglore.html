Bangalore (Bengaluru) is famous for being India's "Silicon Valley" (IT hub) and its pleasant climate as the "Garden City," known for attractions like Bangalore Palace, Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, and the Vidhana Soudha (state legislature). It's also known for vibrant culture, famous filter coffee and dosa, bustling markets like KR Market, and nearby getaways to Nandi Hills and Bannerghatta National Park, blending heritage with modern innovation.
